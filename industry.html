Development 01 — IP & Franchise Strategy
Game industry has fundamentally changed, franchises are no longer commercial operations
0PLN — Witcher products 2022–25
0Book revenue up post Netflix
0Witcher 3 copies sold

Games are no longer just a product. They are a foundation of an entire brand universe and commonly also a studio image. Understanding this trend allows businesses to win culturally and financially, achieving greater results compared to a studio with only game-focused strategy.

CD Projekt Red: Between 2022 and 2025, CDPR's The Witcher franchise related products such as toys, books and tv series generated over 100 million PLN, while Cyberpunk 2077 merchandise brought the company over 70 million PLN.

Over 170 million PLN additional revenue acquired entirely outside the games themselves, demonstrating a great IP management. The Witcher's series launch on Netflix impacted the brand at a greater scale with physical book revenue for the franchise increased by 562% in two weeks since the premiere, further demonstrating how a single franchise expansion can impact the ecosystem of products.

Cyberpunk 2077 has followed the same pattern with a release of the Cyberpunk: Edgerunners anime series on Netflix. With the release of the anime series, the game title increased the amount of players while reaching 20 million copies sold. CDPR's CFO has accredited the anime as proof that risks and opportunities with franchise expansion was the right direction.

The Witcher 3 has now sold 60 million copies with an increasing number due to sustained cultural presence with transmedia expansion support.

With a goal to become a brand creative director within the gaming industry it is crucial to understand the environment and it trends as it redefines the role's position. It is insufficient for a big gaming studio to manage a game's visual identity or campaign assets alone while the industry changes. The position requires to think across different formats above games such as animation or live events with the IP maintaining consistency while understanding the significance of how narrative decisions on one channel affects audience perception across all other channels.

Development 02 — AI Integration & Brand Trust
The introduction of AI has changed the market as well as job roles in marketing and the gaming industry
0Marketers use AI daily 2025
0Report AI increase since 2025
0Companies AI-edit content
90% of marketers use AI for text based tasks, with the most common usage for idea generation, draft creation and writing. AI has impacted the workflow and become standardised.

It is no longer a topic of debate but an expectation and required skill by companies. Over 60% of marketers currently use AI tools daily, whereas only 34% used it in 2024, and 84% report increase of AI usage since 2025. It is crucial to understand what AI is being used for and how it affects the results.

Despite AI efficiency, there is an increasing conflict between public positioning and private practice. Many businesses position themselves in the market as committed and human-led creative practices while discretely integrating AI across productions strategy. Over 97% of companies edit and review content before publishing with the use of AI, demonstrating the standardised practices within the industry.

Due to the increasing gap of companies' public, ethical and responsible use of AI is an important discussion point within the industry. Gaming industry has also been greatly impacted by the AI with increasing number of gamers backlashing when a studio incorporates AI that feels inauthentic and exploitative. As a result, studios therefore face a challenge of AI utilisation while preserving the creative credibility audience expect.

With the objective to start at the junior level, entering the industry, understanding AI and how to use it is a necessary skill rather than a skill differentiator. With the focus on ongoing changes and consumer perception it is crucial to understand how to maintain the brand voice, creativeness and audience trust in workflow that requires adoption of new efficient technologies.
