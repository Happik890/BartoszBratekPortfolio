The Gap
The Starting Point

First Class Honours and three winning live campaign is an accomplishment I am mostly proud of. However, this is only the beginning and the gap is significant compared to where I want to be – Brand Creative Director at CD Projekt Red.

I'm proud of it. Genuinely. But I also know the distance between a good degree and where I want to end up is massive. Being honest about that is actually more motivating than pretending otherwise.
Industry Experience
The Most Significant Challenge

The lack of industry experience is the most significant gap I have to challenge. I have never worked in a professional marketing or creative team. Every project I have completed did not bring a real commercial consequence of failure rather than academic.

During the Channel 4 project I have realised the difference in approach, environment and expectations from a company that expects real outcomes. The campaigns developed for every business did not present me with measurable data such as click-through rates, conversion and audience growth statistics which are crucial in a professional environment.

I haven't worked in a real team with real stakes yet. That's just the truth. University protected me from real consequences and I know it. Getting into a professional environment where something actually depends on the work — that's the gap I need to close more than anything else.

Network
Building the Network

My professional network within the gaming industry is also limited. Within the gaming industry, relationships and personal awareness is crucial especially at a major studio level. To address this challenge and grow on professional level I have attended the Creative Networking and Games Showcase organised by Northampton Film Festival, my first gaming industry focused networking event.

To pursue my career goals, I took an active approach by networking with every studio present and offered my marketing knowledge, skills and time for free. I did not focus on transaction benefit but a step forward and genuine attempt to open paths while gaining real experience.

I walked into that room not knowing anyone and just started talking to people. Offered to help for free. It felt awkward at the time but it was the right move. Not every conversation led somewhere but a few did, and that's enough to keep going.
CDPR Apprenticeship 2025
The Most Useful Experience

Reaching the final stage of CDPR's apprenticeship recruitment process in 2025 was one of the most useful experiences of my career so far. Despite not getting the position it demonstrated exactly what the standards looks like inside, the challenge solutions and creativity the business expects.

This presented me with a clearer path and skills required. Before the process I did not have clarity nor understood what a studio like CDPR expectations were, shaping every decision I made since.

Didn't get it. Still think about it. A lot. But I walked away knowing exactly what they were looking for and what I was missing — and that's more useful than getting lucky. Before that process I was guessing. Now I'm not.
Foundation
What Has Been Proven

My strengths in teamwork, creative strategy, brand narrative and campaign development are evident in real work chosen and executed by clients.

Three campaigns. Three clients who chose our work over everyone else's. That's not luck, that's consistent. It's not industry experience yet but it's real and I can stand behind it.
